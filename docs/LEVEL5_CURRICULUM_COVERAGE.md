## Evidence chain

CURRICULUM OUTCOME -> SKILL -> QUESTION FAMILY -> GENERATOR -> REPRESENTATION -> DIFFICULTY -> HINT -> EVIDENCE

Family metadata is descriptive payload evidence. It does not create family-specific mastery or progression.

## Level 5 to Level 6

The existing diagnostic and adaptive thresholds remain authoritative. These families add varied Level 5 evidence and do not unlock global Level 6 content.

## Deferred gaps

Place-value ordering, fraction ordering and visual comparison, richer area/perimeter diagrams, multi-step data interpretation and equally-likely probability representations remain deferred.
