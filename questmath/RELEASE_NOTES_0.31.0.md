# MathQuest 0.31.0

MathQuest v0.31.0 improves the daily learner experience on tablets and makes Math Mentor support substantially more specific to the question being solved.

## Tablet learning

- Optimises worksheet layout for common tablet portrait and landscape sizes.
- Uses a compact progress summary when space is limited.
- Keeps primary worksheet actions reachable while tutoring content is expanded.
- Preserves comfortable touch targets and avoids unnecessary horizontal overflow.

## More appropriate arithmetic challenge

- Reduces the proportion of simple two-digit addition and subtraction for learners whose recent evidence shows they are ready to progress.
- Introduces more hundreds-based calculations with place value, regrouping and decomposition.
- Retains occasional simpler retrieval items so confidence, prerequisites and spaced review continue to be tested.

## Math Mentor improvements

- Adds structured question context including operation, operands, skill and question family.
- Redesigns Teach me as a short question-specific mini-lesson using the current numbers without giving away the final answer.
- Makes the three hint stages genuinely progressive: nudge, strategy and worked next step.
- Keeps Worked example on the same skill and strategy while using different values.
- Removes the duplicated strategy block that previously appeared alongside the second hint.
- Formats mathematical partitioning as distinct working rather than burying it inside a paragraph.

## Compatibility

- Preserves retry-first answer entry and keyboard autofocus.
- Preserves Visual Mathematics, question-family duplicate protection, fraction number-line fixes and learner-wide completion recommendations from v0.30.1.
- Adds no destructive database migration.
